"Please refer me" is the fastest way to get left on read.

The engineer you're messaging at Stripe or Shopify is putting their name on you. So a good referral message makes that feel safe. One role, like Backend Engineer, job ID 4821. One line of proof, like "cut failed payments 12%". And an easy way to say no.

Asking for a LinkedIn referral? Do their paperwork. Job link, job ID, resume PDF, a 3-line blurb they can paste. Keep the DM itself under 300 characters.

Nudge once on day 7. Silent by day 14? Move on.

Save this for the next DM you're nervous to send.
