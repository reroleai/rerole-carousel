Your best answer is in sentence three. Interviewers stop listening at sentence one.

Someone asks "Why should we hire you?" and most candidates start with where they went to college. The real answer, the 18% or the team of 6, shows up 40 seconds later. By then the interviewer is writing their next question.

The fix isn't better vocabulary. It's order.

Answer first, story second, one ask per message. Same rule for a recruiter email on LinkedIn, a cover letter, or the salary call.

Of all the interview tips out there, this is the one nobody teaches. Five communication skills, one week of practice each.

Save this before your next interview.
