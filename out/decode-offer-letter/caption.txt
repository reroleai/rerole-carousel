Your CTC is a yearly number. Your bank account sees something smaller.

A ₹6 L CTC divided by 12 is ₹50,000. That's not what lands on payday. The CTC on your offer letter also carries your employer's PF share, gratuity, insurance, variable pay and any joining bonus. Some of it comes later, some only if targets are hit, some never reaches your account each month.

Then there are the clauses. A joining bonus can come with a clawback if you leave early. Notice periods and service bonds change from company to company, so read yours line by line.

Before you sign, email HR and ask for the CTC breakup: fixed, variable, one-time, and the monthly figure you'll actually see. It's a normal question. Nobody serious will mind.
