Two candidates give the same answer. Only one of them sounds ready to lead.

The gap usually isn't the words. It's what happens around them: the 2 seconds before you start on Zoom, the pause after you finish, how you take the hard question from the VP on the panel.

That's executive presence. People talk about it like a personality trait. It works more like 5 habits, and you can rehearse every one.

Pick 1 per mock interview, Monday to Friday. Settle first. Let silence sit. Pick a side. Slow down when it's hard. Close with 1 line.

Most interview tips are about what to say. These are about how you hold the room while you say it.

Save this for the night before your panel round.
